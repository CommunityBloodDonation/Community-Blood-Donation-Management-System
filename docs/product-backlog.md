# Product Backlog

## Product goal

Build a web-based Community Blood Donation Management System that helps donors and hospitals coordinate blood requests and donation records while providing public information and secure administrative management.
Under the SE32022 client/boutique crossover, Team 7 develops Team 6's Community Blood Donation application as its product idea. The project follows an iterative development approach, using Scrum practices within the Rapid Application Development (RAD) process to organize requirements, implementation, testing, feedback, and continuous improvement.
The main goal is to deliver a functional, user-friendly, secure, and maintainable application that supports the main blood donation coordination activities.

## Definition of Ready

A story is ready for sprint planning when its client need and outcome are understood, its issue has testable Given-When-Then acceptance criteria, priority and dependencies are clear, and it is small enough to complete in a sprint.

## Definition of Done

A story is Done when its acceptance criteria pass, implementation is merged through a reviewed pull request linked to its issue, relevant security and regression checks pass, documentation is updated, and the GitHub issue and project board reflect the merged work. Record limitations and evidence in the issue and sprint journal.

## Product boundaries and risks

- Eligibility guidance is informational and does not replace assessment by a qualified healthcare professional.
- Do not expose donor contact details without approved purpose, consent, and access controls.
- Do not claim notifications, automated matching, patient accounts, or password recovery as operational unless implemented and verified.
- Keep the backlog, issues, board, pull requests, and implementation evidence synchronized.
