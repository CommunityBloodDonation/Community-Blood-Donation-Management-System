About Us

The Community Blood Donation Management System is a digital platform designed to make blood donation faster, easier, and more accessible.

Our goal is to connect blood donors, patients, hospitals, and blood banks through one reliable platform.

We help people find suitable blood donors based on blood group, location, and availability, while allowing donors to register and respond to urgent blood requests.

What We Do

🩸

Connect blood donors with patients in need.

🔍

Help users find suitable donors by blood group.

📢

Manage and share urgent blood requests.

👥

Maintain donor and patient information securely.

🏥

Support coordination between hospitals and donors.

❤️

Encourage regular blood donation within the community.
