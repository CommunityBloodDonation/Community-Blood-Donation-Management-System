**Project:** Community Blood Donation Management System

## Project overview

The Community Blood Donation Management System is a web application intended to connect donors, hospitals, blood banks, and people who need blood. It includes public information and blood requests, donor and hospital workflows, donation records, and administrator tools. The frontend uses HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with Supabase providing authentication and database services.

## Role and contribution

As UI/UX Developer, I focused on presenting the blood donation workflows clearly and consistently across public pages and role-based interfaces, with attention to navigation, readable information, and useful feedback states.

## Challenge and approach

The same platform needs to serve visitors and authenticated roles without overwhelming each user with irrelevant controls. Organizing content around role-specific tasks helped keep the main paths focused.
